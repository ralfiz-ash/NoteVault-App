NoteVault Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

NoteVault is a notes app for Android and iOS developed by Muhammed Ashiq PP ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data NoteVault collects, why, who processes it, how long it is kept and how you can delete it.

In short: we collect only what is needed to give you an account and keep your notes in sync across your devices, plus basic usage analytics. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and you can delete your account and all your notes at any time from inside the app.

1. Data we collect

DataWhyWhere it is stored

NoteVault does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location, and does not collect or use the advertising ID.

2. How we use your data

  • To provide the app: your account, your notes and sync between your devices.
  • To keep the service secure: only you, when signed in, can read or change your notes.
  • To improve the app using aggregated usage statistics.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use your notes for any purpose other than showing them to you.

3. Service providers

NoteVault runs on services provided by Google, which process data on our behalf: Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Google Analytics for Firebase and Google Sign-In. See Privacy and Security in Firebase and the Google Privacy Policy. We share data with no one else, except where required by law.

4. Security

Data sent between the app and Google's services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and Google encrypts stored data. Firestore security rules allow only the signed-in owner to read or write their notes. On your device, your notes are kept in the app's private storage, protected by your device's own security (such as your screen lock).

5. How long we keep data

  • Your account and notes are kept until you delete the notes or your account.
  • When you delete your account, your account, notes, folders, tags and settings are deleted immediately from our active systems and from the device you use. Copies may remain in Google's backups for a limited time under Google's policies.
  • Usage analytics are not linked to your name or email and are kept for the retention period configured in Google Analytics, after which they are deleted automatically.

6. Deleting your account and data

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. Confirm with your password (or your Google account). This permanently deletes your account and all your notes, folders, tags and settings, on all your devices.

If you no longer have the app, see how to request account deletion.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. You can view and edit your notes in the app and delete everything at any time. For any other request, contact us using the details below; we will reply within 30 days.

8. Children

NoteVault is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date. For significant changes we will also inform you in the app.
