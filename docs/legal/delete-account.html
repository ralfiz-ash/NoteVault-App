Delete your NoteVault account

NoteVault, by Muhammed Ashiq PP

Option 1: in the app (immediate)

  1. Open NoteVault and sign in.
  2. Open the menu and go to Settings.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm with your password, or with your Google account if you sign in with Google, then tap Delete.

You need an internet connection. Deletion happens immediately.

Option 2: by email (without the app)

Send an email to ashiqpp78@gmail.com from the email address of your NoteVault account, with the subject "Delete my NoteVault account". We will confirm that the request comes from the account owner and delete the account within 30 days, then reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your NoteVault account (email address, user ID and sign-in details).
  • All your notes, including titles, content, folders and tags, in the cloud and on the device you use to delete the account.
  • Your settings.

What may be kept

  • Usage analytics that are not linked to your name or email, until they expire under the retention period set in Google Analytics.
  • Copies in Google's backups for a limited time, under Google's policies.

Deletion cannot be undone.
