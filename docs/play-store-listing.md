## App name (max 30)

NoteVault: Notes & Sync

## Short description (max 80)

Private notes with folders and tags. Works offline, syncs across your devices.

## Full description (max 4000)

NoteVault is a simple, private place for your notes. Write anywhere, even without a connection, and find everything again in seconds with folders, tags and search. Your notes sync automatically to every device where you sign in.

WRITE AND ORGANISE
• Create, edit and delete notes with a clean, distraction-free editor
• Never lose a draft: NoteVault warns you before discarding unsaved changes
• Group notes into folders and see how many notes each folder holds
• Add colour-coded tags to connect notes across folders

FIND ANYTHING FAST
• Search note titles and content as you type
• Filter by folder, by tag, or by notes without a folder
• Sort by last edited, date created or title, in either direction

WORKS OFFLINE
• Your notes are stored on your device, so they open instantly and work without internet
• Changes made offline are kept safely and uploaded when you're back online
• A clear banner shows when you're offline and when changes are waiting to sync

SYNC ACROSS YOUR DEVICES
• Sign in on another phone or tablet and your notes are there
• Edits made on one device appear on the others automatically
• If the same note is changed on two devices, the newest version is kept and the other text is saved as a copy, so nothing is lost

YOUR ACCOUNT, YOUR DATA
• Sign in with email and password or with your Google account
• Forgot your password? Reset it by email
• Each account's notes are kept separate, even on a shared device
• Delete your account and all your notes at any time from Settings
• No ads, and your notes are never sold or shared

MAKE IT YOURS
• Light, dark or system theme in a warm golden design
• Choose your default note order in Settings

NoteVault is free. Your notes are stored on your device and in your private cloud space, accessible only when you're signed in.
